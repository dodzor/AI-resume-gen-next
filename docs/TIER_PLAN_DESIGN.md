The trick with pricing is not to hide the main value, but to gate **depth, frequency, and convenience**.

If you lock too much, users won’t see the magic.
If you lock too little, nobody upgrades.

Below is a structure that usually works well for AI tools like yours.

---

# Recommended Tier Structure

## 1. Free Tier — “Try the Intelligence”

Goal:
Let users **experience the insight** so they trust the product.

### Free Plan

**Job Analysis**

* 3 job description analyses per month
* Seniority detection
* Keyword extraction
* Theme detection
* Recommendations summary

**Resume Builder**

* 1 resume
* Manual editing

**AI Features**

* 3 bullet rewrites
* 1 experience improvement
* 1 summary generation

**Keyword Assistance**

* Keyword highlighting
* Skill match percentage

**Export**

* Basic text export
* Copy to clipboard

---

### Why This Works

Users must see:

* “Oh wow it understands the job.”
* “This actually improves my bullet.”

But they will hit limits quickly when applying to multiple jobs.

That drives upgrade.

---

# 2. Pro Tier — “Land the Interview”

This is your **main revenue tier**.

Price range typical for this market:

**$8–15/month**
or
**$29 one-time resume pack**

---

### Pro Features

**Unlimited Job Analyses**

* Unlimited job description pasting
* Unlimited keyword extraction
* Unlimited themes and recommendations

**Unlimited AI Rewrites**

* Bullet rewriting
* Experience improvement
* Summary regeneration
* Section regeneration

**Advanced Keyword Targeting**

* Smart keyword suggestions per bullet
* Keyword coverage optimization
* Missing keyword alerts

**Advanced Tone Control**

* Junior / Mid / Senior
* Role-specific tone tuning

**Resume Management**

* Multiple resumes
* Duplicate resume for different jobs
* Version history

**Export**

* PDF export
* ATS-friendly formatting
* Multiple resume formats

---

# 3. Power User / Career Tier (Optional Later)

For heavy users, job hunters, and consultants.

Price:

$19–29/month

---

### Additional Features

**Job Fit Score**
Not a gimmicky ATS score — but:

* Keyword coverage
* Theme alignment
* Seniority match

**Per-Company Resume Versioning**

Example:

```
Google Resume
Stripe Resume
Startup Resume
```

**Advanced Suggestions**

* Missing experience types
* Skill gaps
* Leadership signals

**Portfolio / Project Suggestions**

Based on job description.

**LinkedIn Summary Generator**

Reuse the analysis.

---

# Feature Gating Strategy (Important)

Do **not** gate these behind paywall:

✅ Job analysis insight
✅ Keyword extraction
✅ Theme explanation

Those are your **viral features**.

Instead gate:

* quantity
* automation
* exporting
* convenience

---

# The Best Upgrade Trigger

Your strongest paywall moment is here:

User clicks:

**“Rewrite bullet”**

Popup:

> You've used your free AI rewrites.
> Upgrade to tailor your resume for every job.

Conversion rates are highest there.

---

# Competitive Pricing Reality

Competitors roughly charge:

* Teal: ~$29/month
* Rezi: ~$29/month
* Kickresume: ~$19/month
* Enhancv: ~$24/month

This gives you room to compete with:

**$12/month** and look like a bargain.

# A Positioning Tier Name Example

Free → **Starter**
Pro → **Interview Ready**
Power → **Career Accelerator**

Much better than Free / Pro / Premium.

# Hidden Opportunity Most CV Apps Miss

Because your product analyzes **job descriptions**, you could later add:

**Job search intelligence**

Example:

> This job is actually senior despite title.
> Your resume should emphasize architecture.

That’s extremely valuable.

# My Honest Product Advice

Your real differentiation is:

**Job → Resume intelligence**

Not:

**Resume templates**

Avoid becoming a design tool.
